# Pasta con Pancetta e Piselli
*Shells with crispy pancetta, peas, and Parmesan — rich, buttery, and easy*

---

## Shopping List

**Produce**
- Garlic (1 clove)

**Meat**
- Pancetta, diced (~4oz / 100g)

**Dairy**
- Parmesan, block (finely grated is best)
- Unsalted butter

**Frozen**
- Frozen peas

**Pantry / Center Aisles**
- Large shell pasta (or orecchiette if you can find it)

*You should have at home: salt, black pepper*

---

## Recipe (2 servings)

**Ingredients**
- 200g (about 7oz) large shells or orecchiette
- 100g (4oz) diced pancetta
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter
- 60g (about 2oz) Parmesan, finely grated
- 1 garlic clove
- Salt and black pepper

**Steps**

1. **Boil the pasta.** Salt your water generously. Cook pasta until just al dente (1-2 min less than the package says). Before draining, save a mug of pasta water — don't skip this.

2. **Cook the pancetta.** Start pancetta in a cold, dry pan — no oil needed. Bring to medium heat and cook until fat renders and pancetta is lightly crispy, about 5-7 minutes. Add garlic for the last minute, then remove it.

3. **Add the peas.** Add frozen peas to the pan and toss with the pancetta for about 2 minutes until warmed through and bright green.

4. **Bring it together.** Add drained pasta to the pan with the butter and a good splash of pasta water. Toss over low heat until butter melts into a light glossy sauce. Add more pasta water as needed — you want it silky, not dry.

5. **Finish with Parmesan.** Take off the heat. Add grated Parmesan and black pepper and toss to combine. Taste before adding salt — pancetta and Parmesan are both salty.

6. **Serve.** Plate and finish with extra Parmesan and cracked black pepper.

---

## Tips & Notes
- **No oil in the pan** when starting the pancetta — the fat renders out on its own
- **Pasta water is key** — the starch makes the butter into a proper silky sauce
- **Unsalted butter is best** — easier to control the saltiness alongside the pancetta and Parmesan
- **Finely grated Parmesan melts much more smoothly** than shredded; use a microplane if you have one
- **Frozen peas are perfect** — no need for fresh
- A **squeeze of lemon** at the end brightens it up nicely if you have one
- A handful of **baby spinach** wilted in with the peas adds nice greenery
- **Bacon works** as a substitute for pancetta — a little smokier but delicious
