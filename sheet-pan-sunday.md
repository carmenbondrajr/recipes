# 🍋 Sheet Pan Sunday
### Italian Sheet Pan Chicken & Veggies

## Shopping List

**Produce**
- Cherry tomatoes (1 pint / ~2 cups)
- Zucchini
- Bell peppers
- Red onion
- Lemon

**Meat**
- Chicken cutlets (4 pieces)

**Pantry / Center Aisles**
- Minced garlic (jar)

*You should have at home: olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder, Italian seasoning, paprika*

---

## Recipe

**Ingredients**
- 4 chicken cutlets
- ~2 cups cherry tomatoes
- 1 zucchini, chopped
- 1–2 bell peppers, chopped
- ½ red onion, cut into chunks
- 3–4 tbsp olive oil
- 2–4 tsp minced garlic (split between chicken and veggies)
- 1 tsp salt (or more — season aggressively)
- Black pepper, garlic powder, Italian seasoning, paprika
- Lemon, for finishing

**Steps**

1. **Preheat.** 425°F.

2. **Season the chicken.** Mix **1–2 tsp garlic** into **~2 tbsp olive oil** first so it distributes evenly. Coat the **4 cutlets**, then season aggressively with **1 tsp salt**, pepper, garlic powder, Italian seasoning, and paprika. Spread on one pan with nothing overlapping.

3. **Prep the veggies.** Toss the **~2 cups cherry tomatoes**, **1 chopped zucchini**, **1–2 chopped bell peppers**, and **½ red onion** with the remaining **~2 tbsp olive oil**, **1–2 tsp garlic**, and the same seasonings. Spread on a second pan with nothing overlapping.

4. **Roast.** Place both pans side by side on the same oven rack. Roast 20–25 minutes. Rotate both pans halfway through (around 10–12 min) — swap left/right and turn 180° to account for hot spots.

5. **Check.** Chicken is done at 165°F internal temp. Check at 20 minutes.

6. **Finish.** Squeeze the **fresh lemon** over everything when it comes out, or add a pat of butter on each piece while still hot.

---

## Tips & Notes
- **Season more than feels right** — about ¾ tsp salt per pound of chicken. It should look like you overdid it before it goes in.
- **Mix garlic into the oil first** — bare garlic bits on a hot pan will burn and go bitter
- **Spread everything out** — overlapping means steaming, not roasting
- **Prep ahead:** season and cover the pans in the morning, refrigerate, then just throw them in when you get home
