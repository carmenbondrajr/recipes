# 🍲 Pasta Fazool
### Stovetop Pasta Fagioli

*One 10-inch Dutch oven, ~55 minutes, makes about 4 quarts (roughly 5 generous servings). Ham and pepperoni render into an orange, smoky base, three kinds of beans make it hearty, and the pasta gets cooked separately so the soup stays soup.*

## Shopping List

**Produce**
- 1 yellow onion
- Baby carrots (2 cups, about half a 1 lb bag)
- Celery (4 stalks, about half a bunch)
  - *Shortcut: a tub of pre-diced mirepoix instead of chopping onion, carrot, and celery*

**Meat / Deli**
- Ham steak (14 oz)
- Pepperoni (5 oz stick or bag)

**Dairy**
- Grated parmesan or pecorino (½ cup, plus more for serving)

**Pantry / Center Aisles**
- Petite diced tomatoes (one 28 oz can)
- Chicken broth (one 32 oz carton). 3 cups go in the pot, and the last cup is for thinning leftovers
- Chickpeas (one 15 oz can)
- Kidney beans (one 15 oz can)
- Cannellini beans (one 15 oz can)
- Ditalini pasta (one 1 lb box). Any tiny tube or shell works if they're out

*You should have at home: olive oil, garlic powder, dried basil, kosher salt, black pepper, hot sauce*

---

## Recipe

**Ingredients**

*The base*
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 14 oz ham steak, cubed
- 5 oz pepperoni, cut into coins then quartered
- 1 yellow onion, diced

*The pot*
- 2 cups baby carrots, cut into coins
- 4 celery stalks, diced
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 3 cups chicken broth (save the last cup from the carton)
- 1 can (28 oz) petite diced tomatoes, undrained
- 1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 can (15 oz) kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 can (15 oz) cannellini beans, drained and rinsed

*To finish*
- ½ cup grated parmesan or pecorino
- Hot sauce, to taste
- Ditalini, cooked separately, only what you're eating that day

### 1️⃣ Render the ham & pepperoni · ⏱ ~8 min
- Heat the **2 tbsp olive oil** in the Dutch oven over medium for a couple of minutes first. A heavy pot heats unevenly if you rush it.
- Add the **14 oz cubed ham** and **5 oz quartered pepperoni** and cook until the fat renders and the pepperoni edges curl. The oil goes orange, and that orange oil is the flavor base of the whole soup.
- Stir every couple of minutes, and watch the **pot bottom**, not the meat. A light brown crust on the bottom is the goal.
- ⚠️ **Don't let the bottom scorch.** Overcooked meat isn't the risk here, black fond is, and it turns the whole pot bitter. A Dutch oven holds so much heat that turning the burner down takes minutes to work, so if it starts going dark, splash in a little broth right away and scrape it up.

### 2️⃣ Soften the onion · ⏱ ~5 min
- Add the **diced yellow onion** and cook until translucent.
- Scrape the bottom as you go. The onion's moisture lifts the fond, so the browned bits end up in the soup instead of stuck to the pot.

### 3️⃣ Sweat the vegetables · ⏱ ~4 min
- Add the vegetables and seasonings:
  - **2 cups carrot coins**
  - **4 diced celery stalks**
  - **1 tsp garlic powder**
  - **1 tsp dried basil**
  - **1 tsp kosher salt**
  - **½ tsp black pepper**
- Stir for a few minutes until the celery brightens. Toasting the dried spices in that orange fat wakes them up before any liquid goes in.

### 4️⃣ Simmer · ⏱ ~25 min
- Pour in the **3 cups chicken broth** and scrape the bottom clean.
- Add the **28 oz can of tomatoes** and all three cans of beans (**chickpeas, kidney, cannellini**, drained and rinsed), then stir it all together.
- Bring it to a boil, then drop to low so it just barely bubbles. Set the lid on **slightly ajar**.
- Simmer about **20 minutes**, until a carrot coin squishes easily against the side of the pot. The carrots set the timer. The canned beans are already cooked, so they just need to heat through and soak up flavor.
- ⚠️ **Stir and scrape the bottom every 5 minutes.** Beans and tomato settle as it simmers and can quietly scorch on the bottom of a heavy pot.
- ⚠️ **Keep it at a bare simmer.** The pot is about ¾ full, so a hard boil will splash over the rim, especially with the lid on.

### 5️⃣ Cook the pasta · ⏱ ~10 min
- While the soup simmers, boil the **ditalini** in salted water in a separate pot. Plan on about **¼ cup dry per bowl**. It takes about 8 minutes.
- Drain and rinse it under cold water. Rinsing washes off the surface starch so it doesn't clump or thicken the soup.
- Cook only what you're eating today. Pasta stored in the soup keeps drinking broth and goes gluey overnight.

### 6️⃣ Finish & serve
- Taste the soup and adjust the salt.
- Spoon pasta into each bowl and ladle the soup over it.
- Pass the **½ cup grated cheese** and the **hot sauce** at the table.

## Tips & Notes
- **What ruins this: a scorched bottom.** It happens either in step 1, with black fond, or in step 4, when settled beans sit on the bottom too long. Medium heat, a bare simmer, and a scrape every 5 minutes cover both.
- **Never cook the pasta in the soup, and never store it in the soup.** It swells, goes gluey, and soaks up all the broth.
- **Don't simmer much past 30 minutes.** The canned beans start splitting and the cannellini go mealy. Once the carrots are tender, you're done.
- **Leftovers:** keeps 5 days in the fridge and is better on day two. The beans soak up broth overnight, so thin it out with the saved **1 cup of broth** when you reheat. Freezes well in quart containers, without the pasta.
- **Using a bigger pot (6 qt or more)?** Go back to the full batch: **6 celery stalks** and the whole **4 cups of broth** in the pot.
- **Want it creamier?** Mash a ladle of the beans against the side of the pot after cooking and stir them back in. The starch thickens the broth.
- **The name:** *pasta e fagioli* just means "pasta and beans." In Neapolitan dialect, *fagioli* is *fasule*, which Italian-American kitchens turned into "fazool." Dean Martin made it famous in "That's Amore": *"When the stars make you drool just like pasta e fazool."*
