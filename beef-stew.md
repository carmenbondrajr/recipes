# 🥘 Stovetop Beef Stew

## Shopping List

**Produce**
- Mini yellow potatoes (use about half a 3lb bag)
- 3 carrots, cut into 1" pieces
- 3 celery stalks, sliced
  - don't slice these small like you normally would, leave em bigger for a crunch
- 1 onion (yellow or Vidalia), diced
- 3 cloves garlic

**Meat**
- 2 lbs beef stew meat (round), cut into 1.5" chunks
  - beef tips may be better for next time

**Frozen**
- Bag of frozen mixed veggies

**Pantry / Center Aisles**
- Beef broth (1 carton / 32 oz — you'll use 3 cups, keep the rest in reserve)
- Small can tomato paste (you'll use ~3 tbsp)
- 1 chicken bouillon cube or tsp paste
- Worcestershire sauce (if you don't have it)

*You should have at home: olive oil, flour for dredging, bay leaves, thyme, rosemary, paprika, salt, pepper*

---

## Recipe

### 1. Season & Dredge
Pat the **2 lbs beef** dry, season with salt and pepper, toss in flour until lightly coated.

### 2. Brown the Beef
Heat olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high. Sear beef in batches (don't crowd!) until deeply browned on all sides, ~3-4 min per side. Rest on a plate. Add a small splash of oil between batches as needed. **Tip: do the oil + butter trick** — add a knob of butter right at the very end of each batch for extra flavor.

### 3. Sauté Aromatics
Reduce to medium. Cook the **diced onion** and **3 sliced celery stalks** ~5 min, scraping up the browned bits. Add the **3 cloves garlic**, cook 1 min. Add **~3 tbsp tomato paste**, cook 2 min to caramelize.

### 4. Build the Broth
Add **3 cups beef broth**, scraping up any remaining fond. Add the **bouillon cube**, paprika, thyme, rosemary, bay leaves. Add 3-4 tsp Worcestershire. Taste before adding salt — bouillon is salty.

### 5. Simmer the Beef
Return beef and any resting juices to the pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a lazy simmer (not a rolling boil). Cover with lid slightly ajar. Simmer ~1 hour, stirring every 15-20 min. Beef is ready when a fork yields easily and a bite chews without toughness.

### 6. Add Vegetables
Add the **potatoes (~1.5 lbs, halved)** and **3 carrots**. Simmer another 40-50 min until fork-tender. Don't panic if it needs more time — just keep going. Dump in the **bag of frozen mixed veggies** in the last 10 minutes. 

### 7. Finish
Remove bay leaves. Mash a few potatoes against the side to thicken naturally, or stir in a cornstarch slurry (1 tbsp cornstarch + 2 tbsp cold water) and simmer uncovered 5-10 min. Taste and adjust seasoning.

---

## Tips
- **Dry the beef before searing** — moisture is the enemy of a good crust
- **Work in batches** — crowding steams instead of sears
- **Don't skip caramelizing the tomato paste** — it deepens flavor significantly
- **Pour resting juices back in** when you return the beef to the pot
- **Keep reserved broth handy** to add if the stew reduces too much
- **Tart broth?** It mellows over time. If still tart at the end, a pinch of sugar or knob of butter fixes it
- **Vidalia onions work great** — slightly sweeter, balances the savory broth nicely
- **Low and slow** — aggressive boiling toughens the meat

## Flavor Upgrades for Next Time
- 1 tsp soy sauce or balsamic vinegar in the broth (great Worcestershire sub)
- Season the beef more aggressively before dredging
