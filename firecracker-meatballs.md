*HelloFresh recipe — tweaked over time*

## Recipe (2 servings)

**Ingredients**

*Meatballs*
- 1 lb ground beef
- ¾ cup panko breadcrumbs
- 3–4 thumbs fresh ginger, minced (or 3–4 tbsp ginger paste)
- 3 scallions, whites minced, greens sliced (save greens for garnish)
- 2 tsp soy sauce
- Salt and pepper

*Sauce*
- 3 tbsp mayonnaise
- 5 tbsp sour cream
- 4 tsp honey
- 4 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sriracha

*Rice*
- 1 cup jasmine rice
- 1 cup water
- 1/2 tsp salt

*Green beans*
- 1 steamable bag of green beans

*Garnish*
- Scallion greens

**Steps**

1. **Prep.** Preheat oven to 425°F, top rack. Slice scallions — separate whites from greens. Mince whites and ginger. Rinse 1 cup jasmine rice under cold water in a mesh strainer and leave it there to drain.

2. **Form meatballs.** Mix beef, ¾ cup panko, ginger, scallion whites, 2 tsp soy sauce, salt, and pepper in a large mixing bowl. Form into ~14 meatballs — keep them on the smaller side.

3. **Rice and meatballs go at the same time.** Rinsed rice into the Instant Pot with 1 cup water and a pinch of salt — high pressure 3 min, valve sealed, then natural release 10 min (~20 min total: 7 to pressure + 3 cooking + 10 release). Hit start, then spread the meatballs across a fully oiled sheet pan and roast on the top rack 13 min, then look — if they're not browned enough, give them another 2. *(Optional: shake the pan or flip halfway through for more even browning.)*

4. **While everything cooks — make sauce and microwave green beans.** Combine 3 tbsp mayo, 5 tbsp sour cream, 4 tsp honey, 4 tsp soy sauce, and 1 tsp sriracha in a medium mixing bowl. Microwave green bean bag according to package directions.

5. **Serve.** Fluff rice, divide into bowls. Toss meatballs in the sauce, place on rice. Green beans on the side. Garnish with scallion greens.

## Tips & Notes

- **Ginger paste subs 1:1 by volume.** One thumb of fresh ginger ≈ 1 tbsp minced, so use 3–4 tbsp paste. Paste is ground finer, so it reads a touch more potent and less floral than fresh — start at 3 tbsp if you're unsure.
- **Paste adds moisture, fresh adds texture.** If the meat mix feels slack after adding paste, work in another spoonful or two of panko. Slack mix = meatballs that slump and steam on the pan instead of browning.
- **Rice is the forgiving one.** It holds fine in the Instant Pot after natural release, so it's better for the rice to be a few minutes early than for the meatballs to sit. If the two drift apart, let the rice wait.
- **Don't skip oiling the sheet pan.** These stick hard, and scraping them off shreds the crust that holds the sauce.
