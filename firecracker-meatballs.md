# 🔥 Firecracker Meatballs
*HelloFresh recipe — tweaked over time*

## Recipe (2 servings)

**Ingredients**

*Meatballs*
- 1 lb ground beef
- ¾ cup panko breadcrumbs
- 3–4 thumbs fresh ginger, minced
- 3 scallions, whites minced, greens sliced (save greens for garnish)
- 2 tsp soy sauce
- Salt and pepper

*Sauce*
- 3 tbsp mayonnaise
- 5 tbsp sour cream
- 4 tsp honey
- 4 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sriracha

*Rice*
- 1 cup jasmine rice
- 1 cup water
- 1/2 tsp salt

*Green beans*
- 1 steamable bag of green beans

*Garnish*
- Scallion greens

**Steps**

1. **Prep.** Preheat oven to 425°F, top rack. Slice scallions — separate whites from greens. Mince whites and ginger.

2. **Start rice (Instant Pot).** Rinse 1 cup jasmine rice under cold water with a mesh strainer. Add to Instant Pot with 1 cup water and a pinch of salt. High pressure for 3 min, valve sealed. Natural release for 10 min, then open. (~20 min total: 7 to pressure + 3 cooking + 10 release.)

3. **Form meatballs.** While rice pressurizes — mix beef, ¾ cup panko, ginger, scallion whites, 2 tsp soy sauce, salt, and pepper in a large mixing bowl. Form into ~14 meatballs — keep them on the smaller side.

4. **Roast.** Spread meatballs across a fully oiled sheet pan. Roast on top rack 15–16 min until cooked through. *(Optional: shake the pan or flip halfway through for more even browning.)*

5. **While meatballs roast — make sauce and microwave green beans.** Combine 3 tbsp mayo, 5 tbsp sour cream, 4 tsp honey, 4 tsp soy sauce, and 1 tsp sriracha in a medium mixing bowl. Microwave green bean bag according to package directions.

6. **Serve.** Fluff rice, divide into bowls. Toss meatballs in the sauce, place on rice. Green beans on the side. Garnish with scallion greens.
