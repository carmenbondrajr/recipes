# 🔥 Firecracker Meatballs

*Serves 2, ~35 minutes. Gingery roasted beef meatballs tossed in a sweet-spicy creamy sauce, over Instant Pot jasmine rice with green beans. A HelloFresh recipe, tweaked over time.*

## Shopping List

**Produce**
- Scallions (3)
- **Ginger:** 3–4 thumbs fresh, *or* a tube/jar of ginger paste (you'll use 3–4 tbsp)
  - Fresh tastes brighter and more floral. Paste is easier and a little punchier
- Green beans (one steamable bag, ~12 oz)

**Meat**
- Ground beef (1 lb)

**Dairy**
- Sour cream (smallest tub, 8 oz — you'll use 5 tbsp)

**Pantry / Center Aisles**
- Jasmine rice (you'll use 1 cup)
- Panko breadcrumbs (you'll use ¾ cup)
- Soy sauce
- Honey
- Sriracha
- Mayonnaise

*You should have at home: kosher salt, black pepper, neutral oil (for the sheet pan)*

---

## Recipe

**Ingredients**

*Meatballs*
- 1 lb ground beef
- ¾ cup panko breadcrumbs
- 3–4 thumbs fresh ginger, minced (or 3–4 tbsp ginger paste)
- 3 scallions, whites minced, greens sliced (greens are the garnish)
- 2 tsp soy sauce
- Salt and pepper

*Sauce*
- 3 tbsp mayonnaise
- 5 tbsp sour cream
- 4 tsp honey
- 4 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sriracha

*Sides*
- 1 cup jasmine rice
- 1 cup water
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 steamable bag green beans

---

### 1️⃣ Prep · ⏱ 10 min
- Preheat the oven to **425°F** with a rack in the top position. The top of the oven is hottest, which is what browns the meatballs instead of just cooking them through.
- Rinse the **1 cup jasmine rice** in a mesh strainer under cold water until it runs mostly clear, then leave it there to drain. Rinsing washes off loose surface starch, which is what makes rice gummy.
- Slice the **3 scallions**, keeping whites and greens separate. Set the greens aside for garnish.
- Peel the **3–4 thumbs ginger** and cut it into thin coins. Pulse it with the **scallion whites** in the mini food processor on **Chop**, scraping down once, until finely minced. Cutting coins first shortens the ginger's fibers, so the blade doesn't end up with stringy bits. (Using **3–4 tbsp ginger paste** instead? Just mince the whites by hand.)
- Oil a sheet pan generously, edge to edge.
- ⚠️ **Don't skimp on the oil.** These stick hard, and prying them loose tears off the browned crust that the sauce clings to.

### 2️⃣ Form the meatballs · ⏱ 5 min
- In a large bowl, mix with your hands until just combined:
  - **1 lb ground beef**
  - **¾ cup panko**
  - **The minced ginger**
  - **The minced scallion whites**
  - **2 tsp soy sauce**
  - **A good pinch of salt and pepper**
- Roll into **about 14 meatballs**, on the smaller side. Small ones brown more before the middle is done, and you get more crust per bite.
- ⚠️ **If the mix feels wet and slack** (it happens with ginger paste), work in another spoonful or two of panko. Slack meatballs slump and steam on the pan instead of browning.
- Overmixing makes them dense and bouncy, so stop as soon as everything is evenly distributed.

### 3️⃣ Start the rice and roast the meatballs · ⏱ ~20 min
- Put the rinsed rice in the Instant Pot with **1 cup water** and **½ tsp salt**. Seal the valve and cook on **high pressure for 3 minutes**, then let it **natural release for 10 minutes**. That's about 20 minutes total, counting the ~7 it takes to come to pressure.
- Hit start, then spread the meatballs across the oiled sheet pan with space between them, and roast on the top rack for **13 minutes**.
- Check them at 13. If they're not browned enough, give them another **2 minutes**.
- 🌡️ **Done at 160°F** in the center of a meatball.

### 4️⃣ Make the sauce and cook the green beans · ⏱ 5 min (while everything cooks)
- In a medium bowl big enough to toss the meatballs in, whisk together:
  - **3 tbsp mayonnaise**
  - **5 tbsp sour cream**
  - **4 tsp honey**
  - **4 tsp soy sauce**
  - **1 tsp sriracha**
- Taste it. It should be sweet, salty, and gently warm. More sriracha if you want it louder.
- Microwave the **green bean bag** according to the package directions.

### 5️⃣ Toss and serve
- Fluff the rice and divide it between bowls.
- Tip the hot meatballs into the sauce bowl and toss to coat. Tossing in the bowl, not the pan, keeps the sour cream from splitting over direct heat.
- Pile the meatballs on the rice with the green beans alongside, and scatter the **scallion greens** over the top.

## Tips & Notes
- **What ruins this: meatballs that steam instead of brown.** Usually it's a wet mix, a crowded pan, or a lower rack. Keep them small, spaced out, and up top.
- **Ginger paste subs 1:1 by volume.** One thumb of fresh ginger ≈ 1 tbsp minced, so use 3–4 tbsp paste. Paste is ground finer and reads a touch stronger and less floral, so start at 3 tbsp if you're unsure.
- **Let the rice wait, not the meatballs.** Rice holds fine in the Instant Pot after release, but meatballs cool and firm up fast. If the timing drifts, let the rice sit.
- **Want more even browning?** Shake the pan or flip the meatballs halfway through roasting.
- **The dry panko does the panade's job.** Instead of being soaked in milk first, it soaks up the meat juices as the meatballs cook, which keeps them tender rather than tight.
- **Sriracha is named after a town.** Si Racha is a coastal town in Thailand, home of the original sauce. The rooster-bottle version most of us know was created in Los Angeles by David Tran, a Vietnamese-American immigrant, in 1980, and named in its honor.
