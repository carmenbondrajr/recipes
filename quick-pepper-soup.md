# 🫑 Quick Pepper Soup
### Instant Pot Stuffed Pepper Soup

## Shopping List

**Produce**
- Bell peppers (2, any color)
- Celery
- Yellow onion
- Garlic

**Meat**
- Ground turkey (1 lb)

**Pantry / Center Aisles**
- Diced tomatoes (1 can, 15 oz)
- Tomato sauce (1 can, 15 oz)
- Chicken broth (32 oz carton)
- Jasmine rice (bag — you'll use 1 cup)

*You should have at home: Italian seasoning, paprika, salt, pepper*

---

## Recipe

**Ingredients**
- 1 lb ground turkey
- 2 bell peppers, diced
- 2–3 celery stalks, diced
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can (15 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 can (15 oz) tomato sauce
- 32 oz chicken broth
- 1 cup jasmine rice, uncooked
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper

**Prep (bowls)**
- Bowl 1 — aromatics: onion, celery, bell peppers, garlic
- Bowl 2 — liquids: diced tomatoes, tomato sauce, chicken broth
- Measure rice separately

**Steps**

1. **Sauté.** Set Instant Pot to Sauté. Add the **1 lb ground turkey** and bowl 1 (aromatics). Cook 5–7 min until turkey is browned, breaking up the meat as it cooks.

2. **Season.** Add the **1 tsp Italian seasoning**, **1 tsp paprika**, **1 tsp salt**, and **½ tsp pepper**; stir ~30 seconds until fragrant.

3. **Add rice.** Stir in the 1 cup uncooked jasmine rice.

4. **Add liquids.** Pour in bowl 2 (diced tomatoes, tomato sauce, broth). Stir well and scrape up any browned bits from the bottom — this prevents the burn notice.

5. **Pressure cook.** Cancel Sauté. Lock lid, set valve to Sealing. Cook on Manual/Pressure Cook High for **5 minutes**.

6. **Release.** Natural release for **10 minutes**, then quick release the rest.

7. **Finish.** Stir and taste. Add more broth if it's too thick — it thickens as it sits.

---

## Tips & Notes
- **Pasta swap:** Use 1.5–2 cups dry rotelle instead of rice. Add it in step 3 with the liquids (no separate step). Cook **4 minutes** on High, then **immediate quick release** (no natural release).
- **Scraping the bottom matters** — don't skip it or you'll get a burn notice
- **Thickens as it sits** — leftovers may need a splash of broth
